Why two domains (rackdeck.dev vs *.rackdev.app)?

Customer tunnel traffic lives on *.rackdev.app while the brand, installer, and control plane live on rackdeck.dev. That isolates browser origins and cookies so no tenant subdomain can ever touch rackdeck.dev.

Does Rackdeck pool VRAM across my Mac and NVIDIA rig?

No. GPU VRAM and Apple Silicon unified memory are reported accurately per host. Rackdeck gives you unified visibility and control over both machines, not distributed tensor sharding.

Can an AI agent connected over MCP open a shell?

Never. The browser terminal is owner-session only. MCP tokens are explicitly scoped to read (health, containers, log tail) or deploy (container actions), and every call writes to the local audit trail.

Can I self-host the Connect relay too?

Yes. rackdeck-relay (3.5 MB) is in the same Apache-2.0 repository alongside Caddy and systemd configs for any $7/mo VPS.
